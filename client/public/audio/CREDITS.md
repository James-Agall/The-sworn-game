Every source is CC0 / public domain, except where noted in "Attribution notes" at the bottom
(horse gallop clips are CC0-derived but hosted on a CC-BY 4.0 listing; credit is given to be safe).

## Attribution notes

- Horse gallop clips: the OpenGameArt listing "Horse gallop on different surfaces" (congusbongus,
  https://opengameart.org/content/horse-gallop-on-different-surfaces) bundles CC0 and CC-BY 4.0 derived clips and
  is tagged CC-BY 4.0 as a whole. Only the three clips whose credits.txt names CC0 originals were used
  (D4XX, StephenSaldanha, MrFizzywig). Credit is nonetheless given here (CC-BY 4.0 style): "Horse gallop clips
  adapted by congusbongus from freesound.org recordings by D4XX, StephenSaldanha and MrFizzywig". Remove these
  three clips if strict CC0-only is required.
- Everything else is CC0 and needs no attribution; credit is listed as a courtesy.
