COLONY2D — REQUIRED WORLD PROJECTION
User correction, 2026-11-11. This overrides the earlier mixed-angle references.

X = screen left/right. Y = screen up/down. No isometric world rotation.
Ground, floors, roads, water and roof footprints are axis-aligned squares or
rectangles. Flat tile interiors are opaque; atlas gutters are transparent.
Walls run exactly horizontally or vertically. N/E/S/W joins use up/right/down/
left, with right-angle L corners, T junctions and plus intersections.
Upright facades use vertical posts and horizontal bases. Depth recedes straight
up-screen. Buildings never use diamond foundations or diagonally receding sides.
Roof slopes and triangular gables may exist in the vertical elevation; they must
not rotate the footprint off the world grid. Furniture uses the same footprint
axes. Organic trees/rocks/animals need not have rectangular silhouettes.
Wildlife has NO saddles, packs, tack or riders. Mounted combat units remain a
separate category and may have intentional riding equipment.

Palette/style references establish colors, shading and character treatment ONLY.
Do not inherit their old perspective. Inspect the generated result, not merely
the prompt. Grid packing alone does not prove correct world projection.
Before runtime integration verify actual cell dimensions, pivots and tile joins.
