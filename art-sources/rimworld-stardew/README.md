# RimWorld / Stardew art sources (sliced atlases)

Prep only. Nothing here is wired into the game. Source: three ChatGPT-generated atlases (copied unchanged to `originals/`, 1536x1024 each). Atlas short names: `villager` (Medieval Pixel Sprite Sheet Atlas), `builder` (Medieval Colony Builder Sprite Atlas), `town` (Medieval Fantasy Town Tileset Atlas).

## Layout
- `sprites/<atlas>/<name>.png` native pixels, transparent background, 1px transparent padding (419 files).
- `normalised/<atlas>/<name>.png` (327 files). Tile/structure pieces: native pixels padded with transparent space to a multiple of 32 in both axes (no resampling). Animation frames: one shared canvas per animation set (same size for every frame, feet bottom-aligned, torso/leg centre-x aligned). Canvas sizes are in `manifest.json` (`normalised_size`, `anim_set`, `anim_cell`).
- `tile32/<atlas>/<name>.png` (61 files). Roughly square tiles (floors, rugs, soil, crops, ground, zones, blueprints, interior floor cells) box-downscaled to exactly 32x32. This IS resampled; use only as a quick stand-in.
- `previews/<atlas>_contact.png` contact sheets (sprites on lavender so transparency and fringes show).
- `manifest.json` one entry per sprite: file, atlas, group, size, suggested_use (SPRITES.md #90-#97), notes (+ normalised/tile32 info).

## Important caveats
- The art is AI-painted and only looks like pixel art. It is NOT on a real pixel grid and sprite sizes are arbitrary (tiles are ~60-110 px native, not 32). Scaling to 32px will soften it; plan on nearest/area scaling and re-checking.
- Transparency: the `villager` atlas had a light checkerboard (removed cleanly via flood fill from the border + fringe clean-up; the sleeping bed pillows were kept). `builder` and `town` have dark blurry vignette backgrounds; sprites were cut with a local-contrast mask + hole fill. Dark outlines are kept, so a faint extra dark edge pixel can remain on a few pieces. A few pieces have a stray 1-2px speck from a neighbour (market_crates_small, hay_pile_crate, cart_produce, villager_eat_table_a_1).
- Not cleanly separable (kept as OPAQUE rectangles including their glow backing, flagged in manifest notes): the 6 zone overlays (`zones`), 4 weather overlays (`weather`), 8 blueprint ghosts (`blueprint`, blue backing), soil/crop tiles, interior floor cells and warm light overlays. Faded roofs (`roof_*_faded`) are very low contrast; their alpha was set to 50% over a soft mask, edges are rough.
- Names are descriptive guesses from the art; the atlases have no labels. Names with a/b/c/_1.._N suffixes mean variants whose exact role (connection-mask slot, open/closed, direction) is unverified. Animation frames are numbered left-to-right from the source row.
- Style mismatch: `villager` atlas uses a curly-haired brown-tunic character; `builder` atlas uses a different straw-hat farmer. They are not the same person.
